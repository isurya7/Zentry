Frequently Asked Questions

How are high-level words detected?

Our system analyzes word length, complexity, syllable count, and linguistic patterns to identify sophisticated vocabulary. Words longer than 6 characters with complex structures are typically classified as high-level.

What happens if I miss a day in my streak?

Your streak resets to 1 if you miss a day. However, you can start a new streak immediately. Remember, every 10-day milestone earns bonus points!

Can I see my points history?

Yes! Visit the Points History page to see all your transactions, including when and how you earned or lost points. You can filter by activity type and track your progress over time.

Are points shared across all activities?

Yes! Points from journal entries, tasks, vision boards, and all other activities contribute to your total points. View your complete points history to see the breakdown.
