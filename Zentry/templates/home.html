Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Zentry

How secure is my data with Zentry?
Zentry uses industry-standard encryption and OAuth 2.0 for all account connections. We never store your passwords and adhere to strict data privacy regulations. Your information remains yours alone.
Can I use Zentry with multiple email accounts?
Absolutely! The free plan allows up to 3 connected accounts, while Pro and Team plans support unlimited connections. All accounts are managed from your unified dashboard.
How does the task point system work?
Tasks are assigned points based on complexity and urgency. Completing tasks earns you points that contribute to weekly challenges and achievement badges. Higher tiers unlock special rewards.
Is there a mobile app available?
Yes! Zentry is available as a progressive web app (PWA) that works on all devices. Native iOS and Android apps are coming in Q4 2023 with additional mobile-specific features.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Of course. You can cancel your Pro or Team subscription at any time, and you'll retain access until the end of your billing period. No hidden fees or complicated processes.
